Before you start

{{ biz.legalName }} uses SIDADI Workspace to run daily work. Here is what that means for your personal data.

  • What we keep: your name, email, employee ID and department. We also keep your daily updates and start and finish times, assigned work, leave, any documents you upload, and your salary and payslips.
  • Monitoring: the admin can see when you sign in and out, whether you are online while the app is open, and the actions you take in the app. We do not record your screen, keystrokes, camera or location.
  • Who sees it: the admin. Managers and accounts staff can see company records (orders, invoices, stock) but not your personal data.
  • Why: to manage work, attendance, leave and payroll as part of your employment.
  • Your rights: you can download your data or ask for it to be erased under Privacy & data, or contact our grievance officer. Payroll records are kept for as long as tax law requires.
