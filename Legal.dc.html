Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-07

1. Who we are

SIDADI Workspace is the internal work, HR and operations app of {{ biz.legalName }}, {{ addressText }}. For this app we are the “Data Fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Our Grievance Officer is {{ biz.grievanceOfficer }}, reachable at {{ grievanceText }}.

2. Who this app is for

SIDADI Workspace is for our employees and authorised staff aged 18 or over. It is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we delete it.

3. What we collect

  • Account details: name, work or personal email, employee ID, department, job title and role. If you sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook, we receive only your name and email from that provider.
  • Work records: daily updates with start and finish times, assigned work and its status, leave requests, and notes from the admin.
  • Activity and sign-in records: when you sign in and out, the sign-in method, whether you are online while the app is open, and the actions you take in the app (for example “submitted an update”).
  • Documents you upload: for example ID or address proofs and certificates.
  • Pay records: monthly salary, paid days, deductions and payslips.
  • Consent records: when you accepted this policy and confirmed you are 18 or over.

What we do not collect: we do not record your screen, keystrokes, camera, microphone or location. We do not track you outside the app. We do not use advertising or analytics tools.

4. Why we use it

We use it to run daily work and assignments, track attendance and leave, process payroll, keep company records (orders, stock, purchases, invoices), keep the app secure, and meet legal obligations such as tax and labour record-keeping. We rely on your consent, which you give when you first sign in, and on the “legitimate uses” the DPDP Act allows for employment. We collect only what these purposes need.

5. Who can see it

  • The admin can see all records.
  • Managers and accounts staff can see company records (customers, orders, stock, purchases, invoices) but not other people’s personal data.
  • You can see your own records.

The database enforces these rules, not just the app screens. We never sell your data.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase stores the database and files, and handles sign-in. It acts as our Data Processor. Data is stored in the region chosen for our project, which may be outside India.
  • Google, Apple or Facebook are used only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply on their sign-in pages.
  • Resend sends one kind of email: an alert to the admin when someone new signs up.
  • Our web host (GitHub Pages or Vercel) serves the app files and may keep standard access logs.

Fonts and software libraries are bundled with the app, so loading a page sends nothing to Google Fonts or other third-party CDNs.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, work, leave, documents and activity records: while you work with us, then deleted within 90 days after you leave unless a law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Payslips and invoices: 8 years, as Indian tax and company law require.
  • Erasure requests: the request record itself is kept as proof that we acted on it.

8. Security

Passwords are stored by Supabase using strong one-way hashing. We never see them. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Uploaded files are private and open only through short-lived links. Access is limited by role. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can:

  • see and download your data (Privacy & data → Download my data in the app);
  • ask us to correct it;
  • withdraw consent and ask us to erase it (Privacy & data → Request erasure);
  • nominate someone to act for you if you die or cannot act yourself;
  • complain to our Grievance Officer.

If we do not resolve your complaint, you can go to the Data Protection Board of India. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened. Because the app is needed for your job, withdrawing consent may mean we can’t keep giving you app access.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will show the new version in the app and ask for your consent again before it applies.
